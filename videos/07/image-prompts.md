```
a young Elamite temple scribe, about 20, lean, short dark hair held by a thin cloth headband tied at the back, short trimmed beard, plain undyed wool tunic to the knees with a fringed hem, reed stylus in hand, wet clay tablet, frightened but focused eyes
```

```
Neo-Elamite soldier, short hair bound with a broad cloth headband tied in a knot at the back, short beard, knee-length belted tunic, large composite bow and quiver on the back, short dagger, no helmet, barefoot or simple sandals
```

**[ASSYRIAN-SOLDIER]** سرباز آشوری (قرن ۷ پ.م)
```
Neo-Assyrian soldier, pointed conical iron helmet, scale-lamellar armor shirt, long curled black beard in rows of ringlets, short kilt, round wicker or bronze shield, long spear, laced boots
```

**[ASHURBANIPAL]** شاه آشور
```
Ashurbanipal king of Assyria, long rectangular beard curled in tight horizontal rows, tall truncated-cone royal tiara with a small point on top and long ribbons behind, heavily embroidered fringed robe with rosette patterns, gold bracelets with rosettes, cold proud face
```

**[TEUMMAN]** تئومان، شاه ایلام
```
Teumman king of Elam, about 50, receding hair under a broad royal headband, short grey beard, rich fringed robe torn and bloodied, wounded by an arrow, exhausted
```

```
Middle Elamite king, long beard, round brimmed royal cap, ankle-length wool robe with heavy fringed hem and a shawl wrapped over one shoulder, bronze mace, gold armlets
```

```
Elamite queen Napir-Asu, dignified woman in her 30s, hands clasped at the waist, short-sleeved fitted gown and a bell-shaped ankle-length skirt with rows of fringes and a decorated hem, bracelets, hair gathered under a fine veil
```

```
Puzur-Inshushinak, Elamite ruler around 2100 BCE, long full beard, round woolen cap, long fringed robe over one shoulder, copper mace, Elamite infantry with spears and tall rectangular shields behind him
```

**[UR-KING]** ایبی‌سین، شاه اور

```
Ibbi-Sin last king of Ur, Sumerian king with a full beard and a round brimmed cap, long tufted wool robe over the left shoulder, bound in copper chains
```

## مرجع ظاهر
- **شوش:** تپه‌ی بلند، دیوارهای خشتی، معبد با **آجرهای لعابی آبی و سبز**، و زیگوراتی با شاخ‌های مسی در بالا.
- **چغازنبیل:** زیگورات پنج‌طبقه، آجر قرمز-قهوه‌ای، تاج آجر لعابی آبی، دیوار سه‌لایه‌ی دور شهر.
- **نینوا:** کاخ با **سنگ‌نگاره‌های گچی-خاکستری** روی دیوارها، دروازه با گاو بال‌دار (لاماسو)، باغ با درخت خرما، کاج و انگور.
- **بابل حدود ۱۱۰۰ پ.م:** دیوار خشتی، زیگورات. هنوز **دروازه‌ی ایشتار نیست** (آن ۵۰۰ سال بعد است).
- **تخت جمشید:** ستون‌های سنگی بلند با سرستون گاوی، پلکان با نقش برجسته.
